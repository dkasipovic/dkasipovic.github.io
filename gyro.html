How it works:
Heading mode — uses gyro compass heading + assumes ~8 m/s (≈30 km/h). Shows the shape of your route (turns) but not exact distances.
Dead reckoning — double-integrates accelerometer. Drifts wildly after seconds — shown for educational purposes. Pinch/zoom the map to see both.
